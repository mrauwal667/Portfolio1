about

I like making complex things feel simple.

I'm Alex — a developer based in the Pacific Northwest, three years into building for the web professionally and many more years into doing it for fun. I started by editing CSS on a friend's band website in high school, and never really stopped.

My focus is the layer where design and engineering meet: interfaces that feel obvious to use, backed by code that's easy for the next person (often future-me) to read.
